# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-07)

First public release.

- 312 chords (12 keys x 26 types, triads to 13ths), with 559 base voicings and all five CAGED shapes for 240 of them.
- 34,829 inversions and compact voicings (dyads, closed and open triads, closed, drop 2, drop 3, shells, rootless), every placement up to fret 22 and every group of strings, loaded on demand. Every voicing is checked by tests.
- Arpeggios, 20 scales on a full 22-fret neck, and a metronome next to it.
- Notes shaded by their job in the chord (root, third, fifth, seventh, extension).
- Circle of fifths with modes, progression builder, study guide (original text), chord quiz, left-handed mode.
- Three themes: Light, Dark and Stage. English and Portuguese. Installable and works offline.
